# PAWFECT
**PAWFECT** is an online pet store website designed to make shopping for pet products simple, convenient, and enjoyable. The website allows customers to explore pet products, search and filter items, manage their shopping carts, create accounts, save favourite products, and place orders through a structured checkout interface.

The project focuses on front-end web development, interactive user experiences, e-commerce functionality, and user interface design using HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript.

## Project Overview

PAWFECT is an online pet store concept designed to provide pet owners with a convenient platform for discovering and purchasing products for their pets. The website brings together different categories of pet products, including food, accessories, toys, grooming supplies, and other essential pet-care items.

The project aims to provide customers with an engaging and user-friendly shopping experience where they can browse available products, search for specific items, apply filters based on pet type, category, price, and age, and view individual product details.

Customers can also create accounts, log in, manage their shopping carts, save favourite products to a personal wishlist, and place orders through a checkout process that supports M-Pesa and Cash on Delivery as payment options.

Inspired by the growing demand for convenient online shopping, PAWFECT combines a playful brand identity, pet-related imagery, a purple-inspired colour palette, structured product layouts, and intuitive navigation to create a consistent digital shopping experience.

The website is currently developed using HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript as a front-end e-commerce prototype. Browser local storage is used to simulate customer accounts, shopping carts, wishlists, and order management, with opportunities for future expansion into a fully functional online store.

## Technologies Used

## Project Goals

The primary goal of PAWFECT is to develop an attractive, interactive, and user-friendly online pet store that simplifies the process of shopping for pet products.

### The specific objectives are to:

- **Simplify online pet shopping:** Provide an organised platform where customers can explore different pet products and access product information.

- **Improve product discovery:** Implement search, sorting, and filtering functionality based on pet type, product category, price range, and pet age.

- **Enhance customer experience:** Develop an intuitive shopping cart, product details pages, and a structured checkout process.

- **Support customer accounts:** Allow customers to register, log in, access their personal account pages, and view their previous orders.

## Business Rationale

**PAWFECT** explores the idea of a digital pet store that brings different pet-care products together in one convenient shopping platform.

Pet owners regularly purchase essential items such as food, toys, grooming supplies, and accessories. Searching for these products across different physical stores or online platforms can require additional time and effort.

PAWFECT aims to simplify this experience by providing an organised online shopping interface where customers can discover products, compare available options, save favourites, and place orders.

From a business perspective, PAWFECT could provide a foundation for a future e-commerce business focused on:

- Selling pet food, accessories, toys, grooming products, and other pet-care essentials.
- Providing convenient online shopping and order placement.
- Reaching pet owners through a digital platform.
- Improving customer retention through personalised accounts and wishlist functionality.
- Supporting different payment methods to accommodate customer preferences.
- Expanding product categories and delivery services as the business grows.

## Problem Statement

Pet owners require access to different products to maintain the health, comfort, and well-being of their pets. However, finding suitable products, comparing prices, and purchasing items from different physical or online stores may be inconvenient and time-consuming.

Additionally, customers may experience difficulties keeping track of their shopping selections, managing previous purchases, and easily accessing their favourite products.

PAWFECT aims to address these challenges by providing an organised online pet shopping platform where customers can explore products, search and filter based on their preferences, manage their shopping carts, save favourite items, and place orders through a structured checkout interface.

The project focuses on improving the accessibility and convenience of pet-product shopping through an interactive, responsive, and user-friendly e-commerce interface.

## Target Audience

PAWFECT is designed for individuals who own pets or are interested in purchasing pet-related products.

1. **Dog owners:** Customers looking for dog food, toys, grooming supplies, accessories, and other dog-care essentials.

2. **Cat owners:** Individuals interested in purchasing cat food, litter, toys, scratching accessories, and grooming products.

3. **Other pet owners:** Customers who own rabbits, birds, fish, and other domestic animals and require suitable products.

4. **New pet owners:** Individuals looking for essential products and accessories when preparing to welcome a new pet.

5. **Pet enthusiasts:** People interested in discovering new pet products, accessories, and care essentials.

## Key Functionalities

The main interactive features implemented in PAWFECT include:

1. **Product Search:** Allows customers to search for products by name.
2. **Product Filtering:** Enables filtering based on pet type, category, price range, and age.
3. **Product Sorting:** Organises products according to available sorting options.
4. **Shopping Cart Management:** Allows customers to add products, manage quantities, and remove items.
5. **Wishlist Management:** Allows logged-in customers to save and remove favourite products.
6. **Customer Registration and Login:** Simulates customer account creation and authentication.
7. **Checkout and Delivery Calculation:** Collects customer information and calculates delivery fees based on the selected county.
8. **Order Management:** Generates order references, stores order information, and displays order confirmation.
9. **Customer Dashboard:** Displays a customer's order history and saved wishlist products.
10. **Hero Image Slideshow:** Automatically transitions between different pet images on the homepage.
